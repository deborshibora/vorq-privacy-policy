Section 04

End-to-End Encryption

All private messages and group chat messages in Vorq are protected by end-to-end encryption using AES-256-GCM encryption. This means:

  • Messages are encrypted on your device before leaving it
  • Only the intended recipient can decrypt and read your messages
  • Our servers never have access to the content of your encrypted messages
  • Even Vorq employees cannot read your private conversations
  • Voice messages, photos, videos, and files sent in private chats are also encrypted before upload
  • Encryption keys are generated and stored on your device only and never uploaded to our servers

How media delivery works: Media files sent in private and group chats are temporarily stored in encrypted form on Cloudflare R2 for delivery purposes and automatically deleted within 48 hours after all recipients have downloaded them to their devices. Cloudflare only ever receives encrypted unreadable bytes.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information to any third party under any circumstances. We share information only in the following limited circumstances:

With Other Users

Information you choose to make public on your profile, content you post in public communities, your username in group chats you join, and your online status according to your privacy settings.

With Service Providers

We share limited necessary data with the following providers who are bound by strict data processing agreements:

  • Supabase — database and authentication
  • Cloudflare — content delivery and storage
  • Bunny.net — video hosting and delivery
  • Backblaze — cold storage archiving
  • Google AdMob — advertising to all users
  • Google Play — payment processing
  • Ably — real time message delivery

With Law Enforcement

We may disclose information when required by law. We will notify you of such requests where legally permitted. Due to our end-to-end encryption we cannot provide the content of private messages even if requested because we genuinely do not have access to it.

Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets your information may be transferred. We will notify you before any such transfer and give you the option to delete your account.
